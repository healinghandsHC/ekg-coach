Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

Privacy at a glance

EKG Trainer is an educational reference and practice application. It does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information or user activity.

1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy explains the data practices of the EKG Trainer application. It applies to the Android application and the educational content and features provided within it.

2. Information we collect

EKG Trainer does not collect personal information, including:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contact details
  • Account credentials or login information
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • Location information
  • Contacts, photos, files, microphone, or camera data
  • Personal health or medical information
  • Payment or financial information

3. Accounts and saved activity

EKG Trainer does not require an account or login. Quiz answers, learning progress, viewed pages, and browsing activity are not sent to a server, shared with the developer, or associated with an individual.

4. Health information and medical disclaimer

EKG Trainer provides general educational material about electrocardiogram rhythms. The application does not request, collect, diagnose, or store information about an individual's health.

EKG Trainer is intended for education only. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, clinical judgment, or treatment.

5. Analytics, advertising, and crash reporting

The application does not use analytics services, advertising networks, behavioral tracking, marketing trackers, or third-party crash-reporting services. It does not sell or share user data.

6. Data storage and transmission

The current application does not transmit personal information or user activity to the developer or to an application server. Its educational content, EKG illustrations, quizzes, fonts, and interface assets are packaged locally with the application.

7. Third-party technology and services

The Android application uses Capacitor and standard AndroidX libraries to display and operate the locally packaged application. These components are not configured by EKG Trainer to collect or transmit personal information.

If the application is downloaded through Google Play, Google may process information as part of operating the Google Play service. Google's handling of that information is governed by Google's own privacy policies and is separate from EKG Trainer's data practices.

8. Offline use and hosted services

EKG Trainer's current features can be used offline. The application does not require a Replit deployment, external database, hosted application programming interface, or other developer-operated online service to provide its educational features.

9. Data retention and deletion

Because EKG Trainer does not collect or receive personal information or user activity, the developer has no personal data to retain or delete.

10. Data security

EKG Trainer minimizes privacy and security risks by not collecting personal information and by packaging its current educational content locally. No method of software distribution is completely risk-free, but the application does not operate a remote user-data system that could expose user records.

11. Children's privacy

EKG Trainer does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other user. If the application's data practices change in the future, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect.

12. International users

The application does not receive or transfer personal information from users in any country. Google Play or a device's operating system may independently process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if the application's features or data practices change. An updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

14. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent using the developer contact information provided with EKG Trainer's Google Play listing.
